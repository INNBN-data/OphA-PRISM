# Risk-of-bias assessment status

The author-reported appraisal identifies high risk in the analysis component and overall high risk, principally because of limited event support relative to the candidate search. Penalization and optimism correction do not establish sample-size adequacy.

A complete PROBAST+AI assessment requires the applicable signaling-question forms, supporting records, and separate consideration of development and evaluation. This repository summary is not an independent completed assessment.

Assessment should consider historical selection, formal paired visual testing in 118 of 261 patients, cohort-derived operative target definitions, and the complete candidate search. Temporal predictor eligibility alone does not determine all predictor-domain judgments. The original completed forms require reconciliation with the final manuscript and verified analyses.
